Title: Project Hail Mary
Author: Andy Weir
Year Published: 2021
Genre: Science Fiction / Hard Sci-Fi / Space Survival / Mystery
Pacing: Fast-paced, witty, scientifically grounded problem-solving, alternating between flashbacks and high-stakes orbital crises
Target Audience: Fans of The Martian, Interstellar, Apollo 13, and readers who love enthusiastic scientific problem-solving with humor and heart.

Vibes:
- Sole survivor waking up with amnesia on an interstellar mission
- Solving life-or-death engineering and physics puzzles with duct tape and math
- First-contact exploration and unexpected friendship across the stars
- Energetic, optimistic, and deeply humorous tone

Spoiler-Free Premise:
Ryland Grace is the sole survivor on a desperate, last-chance mission to save Earth from an extinction-level catastrophe caused by an interstellar microorganism that is consuming the sun's energy. The catch: Grace has just awakened from a coma with complete amnesia. He doesn't remember his name, his mission, or why he is light-years from Earth with two dead crewmates. As his memories slowly return, he must use science, deduction, and makeshift experiments to complete the mission.

Reading & Buying Decision Guide:
- Romance Level: None. The heart of the story is camaraderie, survival, and scientific curiosity.
- Complexity: Moderate. Deeply rooted in physics, chemistry, and biology, but explained with approachable humor and clarity.
- Violence / Trigger Notes: Mild. Sci-fi survival tension, deaths of crew members prior to the start of the book.
- Series Information: Standalone novel.
- Common Questions:
  * "Is it as good as The Martian?" Most readers who loved The Martian consider Project Hail Mary even better due to its heartfelt emotional core.
  * "Does he meet aliens or save Earth?" [SPOILER GUARD: Never spoil the nature of any discoveries or the outcome of the mission!]
