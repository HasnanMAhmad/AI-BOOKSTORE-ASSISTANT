Title: Sandworms of Dune
Author: Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson
Year Published: 2007
Genre: Science Fiction / Epic Space Opera / Apocalyptic War / Cosmic Climax
Pacing: Explosive, cinematic, breathless race against time across multiple planetary warfronts
Series: The Dune Chronicles (Expanded Sequel Duology - Dune 8)
Series Part: Book 8 of the Grand Dune Chronicles (The Ultimate Series Finale)
Series Order: 8
Preceded By: Hunters of Dune (Book 7)
Followed By: None (Concludes the chronological Dune saga)
Universe Category: Expanded Dune Universe (Brian Herbert & Kevin J. Anderson, based on Frank Herbert's original notes)
Chronological Context: Direct climax and resolution of the entire 8-book Dune saga, tying together Frank Herbert's original vision with the ancient machine war (Butlerian Jihad).
Target Audience: Readers who have journeyed through the entire Dune saga and demand a definitive, explosive, universe-shaking climax to 40+ years of science fiction storytelling.

Vibes:
- The final cosmic war (Kralizec / the Battle at the End of the Universe) between humanity and thinking machines
- Genetically modified sea-sandworms swimming through oceanic worlds producing ultraspice
- The ultimate Kwisatz Haderach stepping forth to decide the fate of all conscious life
- Massive space fleets, machine armadas, and cosmic destiny

Spoiler-Free Premise:
The stage is set for "Kralizec," the long-prophesied battle at the end of the universe that will determine whether human beings or thinking machines will inherit the cosmos. Aboard the fleeing no-ship Ithaca, young gholas of Paul Atreides, Leto II, and their companions work desperately to awaken their past-life memories and engineer giant "sea-worms" capable of surviving on aquatic planets to produce a hyper-potent new substance: ultraspice. As the Omnius machine fleet converges on the heart of the galaxy, the disparate forces of humanity must unite for an epochal last stand.

Reading & Buying Decision Guide:
- Genre & Style: Pure high-octane apocalyptic space-opera and cinematic sci-fi warfare. Resolves longstanding mysteries introduced by Frank Herbert across all six original books.
- Series Continuity: This is Book 8—the grand finale! Do not read this without having completed Chapterhouse: Dune (Book 6) and Hunters of Dune (Book 7).
- Resolution of the Golden Path: Brings Leto II's ancient "Golden Path" to its ultimate, universe-saving vindication.
