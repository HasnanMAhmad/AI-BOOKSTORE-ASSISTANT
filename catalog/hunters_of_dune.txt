Title: Hunters of Dune
Author: Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson
Year Published: 2006
Genre: Science Fiction / Space Opera / Cosmic Survival / Action Adventure
Pacing: Fast-paced, cinematic, multi-perspective, action-packed space chases
Series: The Dune Chronicles (Expanded Sequel Duology - Dune 7)
Series Part: Book 7 of the Grand Dune Chronicles (Book 1 of the Finale Duology)
Series Order: 7
Preceded By: Chapterhouse: Dune (Book 6 by Frank Herbert)
Followed By: Sandworms of Dune (Book 8 / Dune Finale)
Universe Category: Expanded Dune Universe (Brian Herbert & Kevin J. Anderson, based on Frank Herbert's original notes)
Chronological Context: Picks up three years after the cliffhanger ending of Frank Herbert's Chapterhouse: Dune.
Target Audience: Readers who desperately want the mystery of Chapterhouse: Dune solved, love sprawling space opera chases, and enjoy seeing classic historical Dune characters resurrected in exciting new roles.

Vibes:
- A no-ship wandering through uncharted cosmic voids hunted by an ancient mechanical Enemy
- Resurrection of beloved historical figures (Paul Atreides, Chani, Lady Jessica, Thufir Hawat) as gholas
- Cosmic horror: the mysterious Enemy wielding godlike tachyon nets across solar systems
- High-tech space fleet combat and fast-moving galactic warfare

Spoiler-Free Premise:
Three years after fleeing Chapterhouse into uncharted space aboard the massive non-detectable "no-ship" Ithaca, Duncan Idaho, the renegade Bene Gesserit Sheeana, and their refugee passengers remain on the run. They are relentlessly pursued by a terrifying, shape-shifting cosmic Enemy whose identity was hinted at in Frank Herbert's final notes. To survive and prepare for the ultimate confrontation at the end of the universe, the crew uses genetic material preserved by Scytale, the last Tleilaxu Master, to resurrect gholas of the most brilliant minds, tacticians, and warriors from Dune's legendary past—including Paul Atreides and Lady Jessica.

Reading & Buying Decision Guide:
- Genre & Tone Shift: Noticeably different from Frank Herbert's dense philosophical style! Written in Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson's signature fast, accessible, blockbuster space-opera style with short chapters, rapid POV switches, and intense action sequences.
- Series Continuity: Must NOT be read as a standalone! It directly continues the story from Chapterhouse: Dune and requires understanding Books 5 and 6.
- Frank Herbert's Notes: Based directly on the outline and floppy disks found in Frank Herbert's safe-deposit box labelled "Dune 7."
