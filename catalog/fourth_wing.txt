Title: Fourth Wing
Author: Rebecca Yarros
Year Published: 2023
Genre: Romantic Fantasy / Romantasy / Dark Academia / Military Fantasy
Pacing: Fast-paced, addictive, cliffhanger-heavy, high adrenaline
Target Audience: Fans of high-stakes military academies, dragon riders, enemies-to-lovers tension, and fast-paced romantasy (similar to The Hunger Games meets How to Train Your Dragon with mature themes).

Vibes:
- Brutal war college where failure means death
- Telepathic bonds with ferocious, untamed dragons
- Intense enemies-to-lovers romantic tension
- High-stakes physical trials and hidden rebellion conspiracies

Spoiler-Free Premise:
Twenty-year-old Violet Sorrengail was destined for the quiet, studious life of the Scribe Quadrant, surrounded by history and books. However, her formidable mother—the commanding general—orders Violet to join the deadly Rider Quadrant at Basgiath War College. With a smaller and more fragile physique than her peers, Violet is targeted for elimination by students who view her as weak, especially Xaden Riorson, a powerful and ruthless wingleader whose rebel father was executed by Violet's mother.

- Romance Level: High. Explicit romance (spicy/steamy scenes) and prominent enemies-to-lovers dynamic.
- Complexity: Easy to read, modern dialogue style, fast-flowing prose.
- Violence / Trigger Notes: High mortality rate among cadets, brutal trials, graphic combat violence, explicit sexual content (18+).
- Series Information: Book 1 of The Empyrean series.
